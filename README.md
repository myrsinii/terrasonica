# Terra Sonica

**You are alone in orbit. The radio crackles. Something down there is broadcasting.**

Terra Sonica is a radio you tune from a spacecraft cockpit. Every station is real NASA Earth data, played as music, and shown on a live globe outside your window that you can orbit, zoom and read.

**Play it:** https://myrsinii.github.io/terrasonica/

Made for the NASA Space Apps Challenge by **the blue swifters**.

## Stations

| Station | What you see | NASA record |
|---|---|---|
| Earth's Crust | Air temperature 2 metres above the ground | [SVS 5147](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5147/) |
| Acid Lover | Nitrogen oxides (NO and NO₂) near the ground | [SVS 5154](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5154/) |
| Fūjin | Wind speed 10 metres above the surface | [SVS 5148](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5148/) |
| Nanoair | Fine particles (PM₂.₅) near the ground | [SVS 5151](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5151/) |
| Anything but Albus | Temperature anomalies, 1880 to 2023 | [SVS 5207](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5207/) |

## How to play

Press **H** in the game for the full guide.

- **Space** play or pause, **← →** or **1 to 5** change station, **M** mute
- **Drag** the window glass to orbit, **scroll** or **+ / −** to change altitude
- **Hover** over the planet, or press **V**, to read the data value
- **N** reads the station's description aloud, **L** turns the console lights on or off

Headphones recommended.

## Accessibility

- Every control works from the keyboard.
- Values and descriptions are read aloud on request (V and N) and announced to screen readers.
- The console lights pulse with the music, so the rhythm can be seen as well as heard, even when muted.
- Motion is reduced when the system asks for it, and the lights never flash.
